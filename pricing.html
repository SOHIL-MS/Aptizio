FAQ
Common questions
Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect from the next billing cycle with no penalties.

Is ad spend included in the plan price?

No. Ad spend goes directly to Meta. Our fee covers setup, management, creative production, and optimisation — not the media spend itself.

Do you offer custom plans?

Absolutely. If none of the standard plans fit, book a free call and we'll build a custom scope and pricing around your specific goals.

What's the minimum commitment?

Monthly retainers run month-to-month with no lock-in. Project-based work starts after a signed brief and an initial deposit.

How do you report on results?

Every plan includes a monthly performance report covering reach, engagement, ad performance, and growth metrics. Growth and Premium plans also include strategy calls.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work with clients across India and internationally. All payments are accepted via bank transfer, UPI, Razorpay, and Stripe.

Not sure which plan fits?

Book a free 30-minute strategy call and we'll recommend the right package for your goals and budget.
